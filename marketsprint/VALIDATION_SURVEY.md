# MarketSprint — Validation Survey

## Purpose

Validate the problem, target customer, willingness to try the product and most valuable MVP features before investing heavily in development.

## Live survey

The current Jotform survey is intended to be the primary validation form:

https://form.jotform.com/262468968933073

## Recommended questions

### About you
1. What is your age range?
2. What year of college are you in?
3. What best describes your current stock-market experience?
   - Complete beginner
   - I understand the basics
   - I paper trade
   - I invest/trade with real money
   - Advanced

### Current behavior
4. How do you currently learn about the stock market?
5. What is the biggest difficulty you face while learning?
6. How often do you actively study charts, stocks or market concepts?

### Product validation
7. Would you try a free 7-day stock-market learning + paper-trading challenge?
8. Which features would be most useful? Select up to three.
   - Short lessons
   - Quizzes
   - Paper trading
   - Candlestick/chart challenges
   - Risk-management score
   - Leaderboard
   - Progress dashboard
   - Community / campus competition
9. What would make you come back after the first day?
10. Would you recommend this to a friend interested in markets?
11. Would you like to be an early tester?
12. If yes, leave an email address.

## Sample size

- **30 responses:** first directional signal
- **100+ responses:** stronger validation dataset
- Segment results by experience level and year of study.

## What to measure

Track:
- % interested in the 7-day challenge
- % who select paper trading
- % who select structured lessons
- % who select chart practice
- % who volunteer for early access
- Most common pain points
- Differences between beginners and experienced students

## Strong vs weak signals

**Strong:** "When can I start?", email signup, challenge participation, repeated use, referrals.

**Medium:** feature requests, willingness-to-try, high problem frequency.

**Weak:** generic positive comments such as "good idea" without a commitment to use it.
