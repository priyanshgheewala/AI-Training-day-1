The problem

Students have information. They lack a structured way to turn it into skill.

Finance content is everywhere, but learning is fragmented across videos, influencers, chats and charting tools. MarketSprint focuses on deliberate practice rather than tips.

01

Short lessons on charts, risk, fundamentals and market vocabulary.

Practice

Make simulated decisions with virtual money and see what happened.

03

Improve

Review decisions, track discipline and compete in campus sprints.

MVP

A simple 7-day loop.

Day 1 — DiagnoseTake a 5-minute market-skill baseline.
Days 2–3 — LearnComplete bite-sized lessons and quizzes.
Days 4–6 — TradePractice with virtual capital and defined risk rules.
Day 7 — ReviewGet a scorecard and compare with the cohort.
